## Pipeline Stages
| Stage | Definition | Typical Duration | Actions |
|-------|------------|------------------|---------|
| **Lead** | Identified, not contacted | — | Research, find contact |
| **Outreach** | Contact made, awaiting reply | 1–2 weeks | Email/LinkedIn, follow up |
| **Discovery** | Call booked, scoping needs | 1–2 calls | Diagnose, qualify budget |
| **Proposal** | Sent scope + price | 1–3 weeks | Iterate, handle objections |
| **Negotiation** | Terms/price discussion | 1–2 weeks | Finalize, legal review |
| **Closed-Won** | Signed + deposit received | — | Onboard, kick off |
| **Closed-Lost** | Explicit no / ghosted > 30d | — | Log reason, nurture |
